Key Insights
🔴
Top Risk Drivers
Monthly Income dominates at 74% importance — underpaid employees leave most. Overtime (burnout), Distance from Home, and Number of companies worked are secondary drivers.
🟢
Protective Factors
Stock options reduce risk by anchoring employees financially. Training investment signals growth potential. Longer tenure and strong manager relationships buffer attrition.
💡
Actionable Recommendations
1. Salary review for employees earning <$4K/mo
2. Cap overtime for critical-risk employees
3. Expand stock option eligibility to Level 1–2
4. Launch remote work policy for high-distance employees
5. Increase training budget for young talent
